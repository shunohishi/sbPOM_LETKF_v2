=== Make data in obs. space ===
1. Check and modify
   - [mod_setting.f90]   Modify ndat_a, domain information, and dist_crit
   - [mod_io.f90]        Modify subroutine get_datname, get_grid_size, read_grid, read_ssh_anal
   - [exe_make_data.csh] Modify machine, partition, sdate, and edate

2. Execute
   - csh exe_make_data.csh

=== Validation ===
1. Check and modify
   - [exe_stat.csh] Modify machine, partition, sdate, edate

2. Execute
   - csh exe_stat.csh
